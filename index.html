Doublewires: A Comprehensive Guide

Game Overview

Doublewires is a browser-based game developed by Double Helix Games, released in 2014. The game takes place in an ancient, mysterious world where players must navigate through intricate wire networks to collect and manipulate energy.

Controls and Objective

The objective of the game is to collect energy by manipulating wires, while avoiding obstacles such as spikes, enemies, and broken wires. The game consists of multiple levels, each with its unique layout, challenges, and rewards.

Key Controls:
  • Clicking: Interact with the environment to manipulate wires and collect energy.
  • Scrolling: Scroll through levels to explore new areas and uncover hidden secrets.
  • Dragging: Drag wires to create new paths, obstacles, or items.
